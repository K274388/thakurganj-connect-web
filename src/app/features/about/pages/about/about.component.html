Welcome to Thakurganj

A small town with a big heart.

Thakurganj is a town in the Kishanganj district of Bihar, in the northeastern part of the state, close to West Bengal and the Nepal border region.

Known for its green surroundings, agricultural landscape, tea-growing areas, local markets and close-knit communities, Thakurganj is a distinctive part of the Seemanchal region.

Geography & Location Green landscapes, farmland and connections across the region.

Thakurganj is both a municipal town and the headquarters of a community development block within Kishanganj district. The region has a monsoon-influenced climate, rivers, settlements and green vegetation. Its location near West Bengal and Nepal supports regional connections.

History & Heritage Local traditions, older settlements and stories worth preserving.

Thakurganj is part of northeastern Bihar's wider historical landscape. Kishanganj became a separate district on January 14, 1990. Places of worship, markets and community institutions contribute to local heritage.

Culture & Community Many languages, traditions and celebrations, one hometown.

Hindi, Urdu, Bengali and regional languages form part of the area's linguistic environment. Festivals, foods, family gatherings and the lives of residents, farmers, students and traders shape the town's identity.

Agriculture & Economy Tea-growing areas, local shops and hardworking entrepreneurs.

Agriculture, trade and small businesses are important to local livelihoods. The wider Kishanganj region is recognized for tea cultivation, including tea gardens in the Thakurganj and Pothia areas. Local markets and service providers support everyday life.

Transport & Connectivity Road and rail links connecting people and nearby towns.

Thakurganj railway station serves the local area, while Kishanganj is a regional railway junction. Road connections provide access to surrounding settlements and neighboring West Bengal.
