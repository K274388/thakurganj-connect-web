About Thakurganj

Thakurganj is a town in the Kishanganj district of Bihar, located in the northeastern part of the state, close to West Bengal and the Nepal border region. Known for its green surroundings, tea gardens, agricultural landscape, local markets and vibrant community, Thakurganj is an important town in the Seemanchal region.
